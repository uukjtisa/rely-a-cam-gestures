Round 1, 2026-11-27

  • One program, three situations (top bar). On its own: your webcam, with its own virtual camera and OBS Virtual Camera as outputs. OBS open: OBS actions show up, found through OBS's built-in WebSocket. Inside Rely-A-Cam: the dock's "Open editor..." opens this window, the picture is the phone, and the phone and OBS actions show up.
  • Actions follow the situation. Open a gesture, then Action: only what this situation can do is listed. Switch to "On its own" and the peace sign's "Start/stop recording" stays on it, greyed out with "needs OBS". Nothing is deleted.
  • Try it. Hold "Heart hands": the ring fills, the skeleton and name show, and the meme sits on your head while you hold (itsgiving's way), staying ⅓ s after you let go. Hold "Peace sign" on OBS open: the ring turns amber and asks for the confirm sign. Also hold "Thumbs up" and recording starts. Peace again stops it. "Both palms" is the stop sign.
  • Record a gesture. + Record gesture (or Record take in a gesture) runs 3-2-1, then hold for 2 s. Test shows a live match %, and Misfire check runs 10 s of normal movement (3 s in this mock) and counts false fires.
  • Memes tab: pictures, GIFs and video, sound and volume, placement (on my head, full frame, fixed spot), and "Import from itsgiving folder..." for your own clone's pictures (not shipped).
  • ⋯ menu: Overlays (grid and detection, each for the preview and the output separately), Calibrate neutral face, Settings (outputs, meme sound, confirm and stop signs, OBS WebSocket, start with Windows).
  • Colours are Rely-A-Cam's dock tokens (OBS Yami), so the editor reads as part of Rely-A-Cam when the dock opens it.
